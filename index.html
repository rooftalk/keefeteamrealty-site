Lisa Keefe · Broker Associate, SRES®

Raised west of the Brazos. Still here.

An Army officer's daughter whose family settled west of the Brazos River, Lisa spent twenty years in healthcare marketing before coming home to Texas in 2013. That same year she and her husband Robert restored her great-grandparents' century-old farmhouse, a place other families now make memories in.

Lisa is a graduate of the Texas REALTORS® Leadership Program and chairs the Community Service Committee of the Greater Metro West Association of REALTORS®. When she's not working, she's usually at the barn.
