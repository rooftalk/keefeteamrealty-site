Lisa Keefe · Broker Associate, SRES®

Texas roots. Parker County is home.

Lisa has deep Texas roots and an Army upbringing that took her across the U.S. and Europe. After twenty years in healthcare marketing, she returned to Texas in 2013 and restored her great-grandparents' farmhouse with her husband, Robert. Parker County became home in 2020.

A broker associate with JLA Realty, Lisa works with homes, land, investments and horse properties. She's a Texas REALTORS® Leadership Program graduate and active with the Greater Metro West Association of REALTORS® committees. Outside of work, she's usually with family, volunteering with 4-H or at the barn.
